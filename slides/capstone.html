Format

Ten minutes each: six of walk-through, four of live trace

1 · The decision

Yasmeen's question, the catalogue and the model. One slide each.

Test queries passed, load time, agent accuracy by type.

3 · The live trace

The assessor picks a dashboard figure; you reconcile it to source rows.

Peer feedback. After each review, everyone writes one strength, one question and one suggestion.

The walk-through is six minutes, strictly. The trace is the assessment of authorship: the participant opens their repository, finds the metric in the model, runs its compiled SQL and a hand-written count from the warehouse, and shows they agree. Pick figures that cross a join or a calendar, such as concession passes in a Hijri month.

The live trace

From a figure on the dashboard to the rows behind it

2 · Metricits definition in the catalogue and the model

3 · SQLthe compiled query, run live

4 · Rowsan independent count that agrees

Four minutes. A participant who cannot find the metric's definition in their own model within a minute loses most of this part; one who finds a difference and explains it honestly keeps most of it. The rubric weight is the 20% of the live review in the course assessment.
