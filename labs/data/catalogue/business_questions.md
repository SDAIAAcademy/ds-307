# Twelve business questions (the metric catalogue answers each with one governed metric)

1. How many card taps did the network record last week?
2. How many different cards travelled last week?
3. How many journeys did riders make last week, counting a transfer as part of one journey?
4. How many card-days were there last week (one card travelling on one day counts once)?
5. What share of the trips that departed left their first timepoint on time?
6. What share of the scheduled trips were cancelled?
7. What share of the recorded timepoint arrivals were on time?
8. How many taps were there per trip that departed?
9. How many complaints were received per 10,000 taps?
10. How many trips were in the timetable?
11. What share of scheduled trips reached their terminus no more than five minutes late (the board's measure)?
12. How many timetabled kilometres did the departed trips run?
